Load your IR/UV photo

For full-spectrum converted cameras — JPEG, PNG, WebP, or RAW (CR2 reads the real sensor data; NEF, ARW, DNG & others use their embedded preview).

For the best quality, develop your RAW to 16-bit TIFF in RawTherapee or darktable and load that — a RAW only carries a small JPEG preview inside it, so a TIFF gives you full resolution with no compression artefacts.

The preview is full resolution for brightness, but its colour is stored at quarter resolution (4:2:0). That is invisible on a normal photo and very visible here: swapping channels and pushing saturation magnifies those colour blocks into streaks. Use it for a quick look or light edits; decode the sensor for anything heavy.

05 Colour

Saturation scales every pixel equally; Vibrance weights the boost toward pixels that are still muted.

Tri-tone merge

Three hue-rotated, auto-levelled copies merged by Lighten — a rebuild of the Aerochrome Photoshop action.

Past about 40 the merge starts pulling every hue with it, and near 100 it inverts the swap — foliage turns cyan and the sky magenta. Keep it low unless that is the effect you want.

Split toning

Two tints crossing between dark and bright, added as chroma so brightness stays untouched.
