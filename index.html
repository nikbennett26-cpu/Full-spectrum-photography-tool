Camera sensor baseline
Different colour filter arrays respond differently in the near-IR, so the same filter stack casts differently on different bodies — that part is real, and now sourced: Kolari Vision compiled real transmission curves for 82 camera bodies and found Fujifilm runs hottest for red/near-IR sensitivity, Canon sits in the middle, and Sony/Nikon skew lowest. These profiles apply that real ranking as a directional correction, not a lab-measured coefficient — Kolari published an ordering and a chart, not per-brand gain numbers, so the strength of each nudge is estimated to match the ranking rather than measured. The white balance picker measures your actual camera directly and will always beat this — treat it as a head start, not a substitute. Leave on Neutral if in doubt.
Source: kolarivision.com — spectral analysis of 82 camera bodies
